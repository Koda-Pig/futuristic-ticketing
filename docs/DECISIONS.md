| 2026-11-23 | Bowl fit      | Cape Point's bowl was measured from the shell's concrete treads, not fitted by eye. Rows are 0.8 m deep with risers of 0.36/0.40/0.44 m (lower/middle/upper); tiers start 0, 16 and 28.85 m out from the outline (43 × 62.5 m, exponent 32: nearly rectangular, with the pitch along Z). The middle and upper tiers' rounded corners come from offsetting along the normal, as §6 says. 97% of seats sit within 10 cm of a tread | Eyeballing in `?calibrate` left corner seats metres off; a lower exponent rounds the corners too early |
| 2026-11-23 | Venue schema  | Added `tiers[].rowsByStand` (the ends rake back further: lower 20 rows, middle 16, against 12 and 10 elsewhere; the east middle tier is 0, since it is the model's hospitality seating) and `voids`, plan-view rectangles with no seats (the shell's vomitories and stair landings). `baseHeight` is the front row's tread; seat pans sit 0.45 m above it                                                                        | The shell's terraces are not uniform around the bowl                                                   |
| 2026-11-23 | Capacity      | Cape Point has ≈30.4k generated seats, not ≈34k                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | That is what the shell's terraces hold                                                                 |
| 2026-11-23 | Seat facing   | The generator test allows seats to face up to 60° off the centre spot (§6 said 30°)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | In a near-rectangular bowl, seats near a corner face straight across, as in real stadiums              |
| 2026-11-23 | Far LOD       | One `InstancedMesh` per tier with per-instance colour, instead of a merged geometry with vertex colours                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Same single draw call per tier; recolouring a seat (M4) is one `setColorAt`                            |
| 2026-11-23 | Fly-in pose   | The block fly-in camera sits 7 m above the mid row, not 10 m. At 10 m all 48 upper-tier poses and 4 middle-tier poses were inside or behind the shell roof; 7 m at 18 m out clears every block (raycast against `shell.glb`)                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Upper-tier blocks were a wall of concrete after entering                                               |
| 2026-11-23 | Embed origins | The Netlify deploy-preview origin isn't written into `venue.json`; the build reads `DEPLOY_PRIME_URL` and `MockDataSource` adds that origin to `embed.allowedOrigins` at load. The UI accent (Upgrade button, chosen recliner) comes from `branding.accent` via `--accent`                                                                                                                                                       | Preview URLs change per deploy; Riverside must look white-labelled with no code changes                |
